---
title: Do you want a minion?
tags: [ "ai", "opinion" ]
---

Everybody believes that people aspire to have an AI agent that will do their
bidding -- book their flights, plan their trip, order their meal, etc. because
the people funding it **already** have a personal assistant who has **already**
been granted the permission -- trusted! -- to do everything on their behalf. But
some of us just want to be able to do these things without having to turn the
task into a quest -— I’m happy to pick the food I want, plan my trip… it’s part
of the rich tapestry of life.

Why do I need to delegate it? Are we that inherently lazy? And once we've
delegated it all: What's left?
