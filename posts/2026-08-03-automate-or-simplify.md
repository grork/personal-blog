---
title: Automate or simplify?
description: Why do we want to automate, when we could simplify?
tags: [ "ai", "profit", "ux" ]
---

How much of “AI will automate this away” is about work that is held back by
poor UX, malicious regulatory interpretation, or intentional obstacles to
dissuade leveraging of that work? “Filing insurance could be done by an AI
rather than a health care worker”. Separate from “cost”, why is it so complex
to file insurance *anyway*? Why do we need a complex process around this when,
in reality, it should be much simpler. Much of what is there isn’t inherently
complex, right? What if instead of investing in energy (literally,
figuratively) to “solve” these problems by having computers talk to computers,
we simplify the processes, systems, and regulations, to ensure the right
things happen with the minimal amount of friction, in a predictable and
auditable way. (because there is margin & profit in the opaque)
